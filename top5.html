Top 5 global Charts

1."Billie Jean": The defining track of the Thriller album. Its iconic bassline, pioneering music video, and groundbreaking Motown performance solidified its legendary status.

2."Beat It": An infectious blend of pop and hard-rock, featuring a famous guitar solo by Eddie Van Halen.

3."Thriller": The cinematic masterpiece of the King of Pop's catalog, featuring Vincent Price's spooky narration and one of the most famous music videos in television history.

4."Man in the Mirror": An incredibly uplifting and socially conscious anthem, driven by powerful gospel backing vocals.

5."Smooth Criminal": A high-energy track driven by a pulsing bass and lyrical storytelling, famous for introducing his gravity-defying lean into pop culture.
