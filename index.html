MJ being MJ

Michael Joseph Jackson was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and philanthropist.
Dubbed the "King of Pop", he is widely regarded as one of the most culturally significant figures of the 20th century.
His musical achievements broke American racial barriers and made him a dominant figure worldwide. 



Here are 3 iconic facts about Michael Jackson:
 
He changed pop music forever with Thriller
1.His album Thriller became the best-selling album in history, with estimated sales of over 70 million copies worldwide.
The music videos for songs like Thriller and Billie Jean basically turned music videos into cinematic events.

2.He popularized the moonwalk globally.
Michael Jackson didn’t invent the moonwalk, but his 1983 performance of Billie Jean made it legendary overnight. It became one of the most recognizable dance moves ever — like how one signature move defines a superhero.

3.He won 8 Grammys in one night.
In 1984, he won 8 Grammy Awards in a single night for Thriller, which was a record at the time. Few artists have ever had that level of dominance in music, culture, dance, fashion, and performance simultaneously.
